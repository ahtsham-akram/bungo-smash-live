Our Story

Bungo is where a focused smash-burger menu meets bold Budapest street energy — loud flavors, fast service and zero unnecessary fuss.

Born from a craving

No fuss. No filler.

Great fast-casual brands are easy to understand. Bungo starts with one promise: make the burger worth coming back for. Crispy-edged beef, melted cheese, sharp pickles, a signature sauce and just enough chaos.

From there, everything follows the same rule — the menu stays compact, the visuals stay loud, and the guest experience stays easy.
